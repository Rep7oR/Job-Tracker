JobSync v2.5.0
==============

Home — new Apple-glass design, focused on purpose + presence
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Home is redesigned from scratch around a "Liquid Glass" aesthetic:
  translucent, blurred panels (backdrop-filter blur + saturation) with a
  soft inner highlight, gentle depth and smooth cubic-bezier hover
  transitions — replacing the previous neural-network hub/sector-grid
  visual.
- A new "What this is" glass panel states plainly what JobSync is for
  (a private job-search command center) with three feature tiles —
  Discover, Create, Track — each with a one-line explanation.
- A live "Online now" glass panel shows who else is using JobSync right
  now: an overlapping avatar stack (Apple-presence style, up to 6 plus a
  "+N" bubble), a pulsing live indicator, and the full name list below it
  with its own small internal scroll if there are a lot of people online.
- Quick action shortcuts (Find Jobs, Create CV, Write Cover Letter, Track
  Applications) are kept, restyled as the same frosted-glass material.
- Still fits one viewport with no page scrollbar at typical window
  heights (verified at 900px and 1000px) — continuing the v2.3.0 "Home
  fits one look" work, now on the new design.
