JobSync v2.2.0
==============

CV generation — online AI models by default
---------------------------------------------
- CV Studio's "Which AI should create your CV?" step previously offered only
  local models — picking any of them made JobSync auto-install Ollama and
  download an 8-19 GB model to the user's machine before it could generate
  anything.
- Generation is now online by default: a new model picker offers real
  hosted models grouped by cost —
    Free: Gemini 2.0 Flash, Gemini 1.5 Pro (free Google AI Studio key, no
    billing)
    Paid: GPT-4o mini, GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Haiku, Claude Sonnet 4 (bring
    your own billed API key)
  All of them are called directly over HTTPS (no SDK, no local model
  weights), so the app itself stays a small install.
- The old local/offline path (Ollama + Qwen/Gemma/gpt-oss) is kept, moved
  into a collapsed "Offline / local AI (advanced)" section for anyone who
  prefers it.
- Default selection is now Gemini 2.0 Flash (free, zero local footprint)
  instead of the local Qwen3 14B download.
