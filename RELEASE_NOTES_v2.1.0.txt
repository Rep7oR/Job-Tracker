JobSync v2.1.0
==============

Home — neural network panel redesign
-------------------------------------
- The "Live Opportunity Network" sector cards on Home were plain HTML
  <details> elements — clicking one only expanded a local description in
  place, with no way to actually reach the matching jobs.
- Replaced them with real, clickable sector nodes arranged in a responsive
  grid below a trimmed, purely decorative hub visual (the pulsing core and
  ambient connector dots/lines are kept).
- Clicking a sector now sets it as the search field and navigates straight
  to New Search, pre-filled and ready to run.
- The Online now / Your Progress / Today's Insights rail is unchanged.

Housekeeping
------------
- .gitignore now also excludes data/accounts.json (the local account
  store), matching the existing data/account.json rule.
